Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what NeuralHive collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the service.

What we collect

  • License and account data: your Gumroad license key and the purchase email address and subscription status that Gumroad returns when we verify your license.
  • Agents and chats: the agent names, templates, and instructions you create, and the most recent 50 messages per agent so you can continue conversations.
  • Free preview prompts: the text you submit to the homepage preview is sent to the AI model to generate a response. We do not store preview prompts or results.
  • Rate-limit data: a one-way hash of your IP address with a daily counter, kept for up to 2 days, to prevent abuse.
  • Browser storage: the app stores a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To verify your subscription and keep you signed in.
  • To generate AI responses for your agents and show your chat history.
  • To enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, and keep the service secure.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts NeuralHive, stores app data, and runs the AI models (Workers AI) that generate responses.
  • Gumroad processes payments and subscriptions and verifies license keys. Payment details are handled by Gumroad and never reach us.
  • Web pages load fonts from Google Fonts and page styling from a public CDN, which may receive your IP address as part of the request.

Retention

  • Sessions expire after 30 days, or immediately when you log out.
  • Agents and chat history stay until you delete the agent (which also deletes its history) or ask us to delete your data.
  • Rate-limit counters expire after 2 days.

Your choices and rights

You can delete agents and their chat history in the app at any time. To request a copy or deletion of your data, email launchgrid.hq@proton.me from your purchase email address. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to processing.

AI output

AI responses can be inaccurate. Please avoid entering sensitive personal data (such as health, financial, or government ID information) about yourself or others into chats.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. License keys are never shown to other users, and access to your agents requires a valid session tied to an active subscription. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your license key private.

Children

NeuralHive is not intended for children under 16.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions? Email launchgrid.hq@proton.me.
